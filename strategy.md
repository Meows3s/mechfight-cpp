Movement:

1. prioritize getting on top of buildings and using them as cover from bots below.
2. when in a fight/taking damage, randomly change direction and fly to make tracking harder for the enemy.
3. be mindful of the zone. always be in an area that allows you to get to a safe area inside the zone. (i.e. don't be 10 seconds away from safety if the zone will catch you in 5 seconds)

Attack:

1. try to predict the damage an enemy will do, only engage if we think we can out-damage them and win
2. prioritize mortar weapon, then laser if available
3. prioritize taking out low-health targets in a multi-bot fight

Defense:

1. use cover when possible, don't engage in a fight unless we predict we can win
